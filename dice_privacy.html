Introduction

This Mobile App Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information of users who download and use our mobile application.

Information Collection

To improve the performance of ads, a third party (Google Mobile Ads SDK) may collect certain information from apps, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of a device.
  • Non‑user related crash logs, used to diagnose problems and improve the SDK.
  • User‑associated performance data such as app launch time, hang rate, or energy usage.
  • A Device ID, such as the device's advertising identifier.
  • Advertising data, such as advertisements the user has seen.
  • Other user product interactions, such as app launch taps and video views.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Users will be notified of any material changes.
