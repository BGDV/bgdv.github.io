MTrace

macOS · System Monitor for Apple Silicon

Read the Privacy Policy — MTrace runs locally and collects nothing.

CPU, Net & Memory for M-Series

The essential trio, at a glance. Stop toggling between different monitors. MTrace brings the three most vital metrics — CPU load, memory usage and network speed — together in one clean, persistent view in your menu bar.

Why you'll love MTrace

  • Flexible menu bar. Display 1, 2, or all 3 key parameters at once.
  • Pure efficiency. Built for Apple Silicon (M1–M4) with near‑zero system impact.
  • Visual insight. Sleek dashboard with high‑fidelity gauges and real‑time charts.
  • Minimalist design. Compact Mode keeps your workspace tidy.
  • Seamless workflow. Launches at login and stays out of your way.

Support & FAQ

Thank you for using MTrace! We aim to keep your system monitoring simple and efficient.

Does MTrace impact battery life?

No. MTrace is written in Swift and highly optimised for Apple Silicon to ensure minimal CPU and energy usage.

How do I enable Compact Mode?

Toggle "Compact Mode" in the app settings — it hides detailed activity charts and focuses on high‑visibility gauges for a cleaner look.

How do I enable "Open at Login"?

Open MTrace Settings from the menu bar and toggle the "Open at Login" switch.
