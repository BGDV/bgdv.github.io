Dice for games

iOS · A flexible companion for tabletop games

See the Privacy Policy.

How it works

  1. Dice are intended for any game that uses one to six dice.
  2. The main screen has two menu buttons: Play and Counter.
  3. On the Play screen, simply touch the dice to roll.
  4. On the Counter screen, all rolls are automatically counted. You can reset the counter with the corresponding button below.
  5. Tap the menu icon at the top-left, or swipe right, to open the side menu.
  6. The side menu contains Statistics, Notes, Settings and About.
  7. Statistics displays the total number of rolls per day, organised as a list with the most recent day on top.
  8. Swipe right‑to‑left to delete a day. Tap Undo to restore. Once you leave Statistics, restoration is no longer possible.
  9. You can also delete the entire list at once via the corresponding buttons. A confirmation dialog will warn you the action cannot be undone.
  10. Notes are free‑form. A Delete all button (with confirmation) removes them in one go.
  11. In Settings you choose the number of dice (default: two). The choice is reflected on the Play screen immediately.
  12. You can also enable Summarise and Delay options.
  13. Summarise automatically sums the dropped values on screen.
  14. Delay spawns dice asynchronously with a "twisting" enumeration effect.
  15. Additional features beyond the defaults are available after payment.
  16. Payment is processed via the App Store.
  17. The About screen tells a short story of how the app was created and includes contact information.
